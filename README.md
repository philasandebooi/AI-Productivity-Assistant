# SD Assist: Your Productivity Partner

Create a modern, professional AI-powered productivity web application called “SD Assist” for employees of the Department of Social Development, South Africa. The platform must help employees save time, improve productivity, organise work, and reduce repetitive administrative tasks. It must be easy to use for employees with different levels of digital and AI experience. BRANDING AND DESIGN Use the official Department of Social Development logo prominently in the top-left sidebar/header. Do not distort, recolour, or alter the logo. Display: “Department of Social Development” “SD Assist | AI-Powered Productivity Assistant” Use Department-inspired branding: green as the primary colour, yellow/gold as an accent, with white and light grey neutral backgrounds. Apply colours consistently to navigation, headers, buttons, icons, dashboard cards, active/hover states, progress indicators, alerts, and notifications. The design must feel professional, trustworthy, modern, clean, and aligned with a South African government department—not playful or overly corporate-tech. Create a sidebar with all major features and a professional dashboard. CORE FEATURES 1. AI Case Administration Assistant Allow employees to convert non-sensitive case notes into structured administrative summaries, identify missing information/documents, generate follow-up checklists, suggest next administrative actions, create professional case update templates, and flag incomplete records. AI must never make beneficiary decisions or determine eligibility. Human review is always required. 2. Programme and Project Progress Tracker Track programme/project name, objectives, activities, responsible employees, deadlines, progress status, challenges, and next steps. AI should generate weekly summaries, outstanding task lists, delayed activity alerts, risks/challenges, suggested follow-ups, and management-ready reports. Include a dashboard table showing programme, status, upcoming deadline, and AI alert. 3. AI Report Generator Users enter reporting period, programme/activity, completed activities, achievements, challenges, statistics/results, and recommendations. Generate structured reports with Introduction, Purpose, Activities Undertaken, Key Achievements, Challenges, Recommendations, and Next Steps. Support Weekly, Monthly, Programme Progress, Meeting, and Stakeholder Engagement reports. 4. Smart Follow-Up and Deadline Assistant Track emails requiring responses, outstanding documents, meeting action items, programme deadlines, stakeholder follow-ups, and report submission dates. Display prioritised alerts for urgent and overdue work. 5. Stakeholder Communication Assistant Users select stakeholder type: Community Member, NGO/NPO, Government Department, Municipality, Service Provider, Internal Management, or Programme Partner. Select purpose: Request Information, Schedule Meeting, Follow-Up, Provide Update, Send Invitation, or Request Documentation. Generate professional communication appropriate to the selected stakeholder and purpose. 6. Smart Meeting and Action Tracker Create a Meeting Productivity Hub. Convert meeting notes into AI summaries, decisions, and action items. Each action item includes Action, Responsible Person, Deadline, Status, and Priority. Support the workflow: Meeting Notes → AI Summary → Action Items → Task Planner → Deadline Reminder. Users must review before actions are added. 7. Policy and Document Assistant Allow users to upload/paste approved internal documents, policies, guidelines, and reports. Users can ask questions, summarise requirements, identify employee actions, and create checklists based only on the provided document. Always display: “This response is based on the document provided and should be verified against the original document.” Do not replace official policy interpretation. 8. Document Checklist Generator Generate structured checklists for workflows such as stakeholder meetings, monthly reporting, programme monitoring visits, project planning, community engagement events, and internal meetings. Include preparation tasks, required documents, responsible person, deadline, and completion status. 9. Management Dashboard and Productivity Insights Show tasks completed this week, outstanding tasks, overdue tasks, upcoming deadlines, meetings requiring follow-up, reports due, and active programmes/projects. Include an AI-generated Daily Productivity Insight with practical prioritisation advice. 10. AI Research Assistant Allow users to upload approved documents or paste text from research articles, government reports, programme reports, needs assessments, policy documents, development reports, stakeholder information, and social-development topics. Functions: Ask Questions, Summarize, Extract Key Findings, Identify Issues/Trends, Generate Recommendations, Simplify Information, and Compare Documents. Include buttons: Upload Document, Ask AI, Summarize, Extract Key Findings, Generate Recommendations, Copy, Clear. AI rules: never invent statistics, findings, sources, legislation, policies, or facts; state when information comes from user-provided documents; distinguish AI recommendations from source information; state when information is missing; encourage verification before official use. 11. CENTRAL AI CHATBOT — “Ask SD Assistant” Create a modern professional chatbot as the central workplace assistant. Personalise the greeting: “Good morning, [User Name]. How can I assist you today?” It should assist with drafting emails, summarising meetings/documents, creating action items, planning and prioritising tasks, generating reports, stakeholder communication, meeting agendas, follow-ups, programme/project administration, research, and approved-document questions. Show clickable quick prompts: Draft an Email | Summarize a Document | Plan My Day | What Are My Priorities? | Create a Report | Prepare Meeting Agenda | Show My Outstanding Tasks | Help Me Follow Up | Research a Topic. The chatbot must integrate with other modules. For example, meeting summaries can extract actions and send approved actions to the Task Planner. Meeting preparation can generate an agenda, checklist, relevant outstanding tasks, stakeholder communication, and follow-up templates. Chat UI must include branding, personalised greeting, clean message bubbles, chat history, timestamps, loading indicator, suggested prompts, Clear Chat, Copy Response, Regenerate Response, thumbs up/down feedback, and the ability to send AI-generated content to relevant SD Assist modules. RESPONSIBLE AI AND PRIVACY Make privacy highly visible. Display this notice: “Do not enter personal, confidential, or sensitive beneficiary information into the AI assistant unless you are authorized to do so and appropriate data protection measures are in place. Always review AI-generated content before using or sharing it.” Add an “AI Use Guidelines” section stating that SD Assist may generate inaccurate or incomplete information; users should not enter unnecessary sensitive information; all AI outputs must be reviewed and verified before official communication, reporting, decisions, or actions. The AI must protect privacy, avoid requesting unnecessary sensitive information, avoid unnecessary storage of sensitive data, never make final beneficiary or eligibility decisions, never present AI output as official government policy or legal advice, clearly indicate when human verification is required, and remain professional, objective, practical, and workplace-focused. Build the prototype with realistic sample dashboard data, polished interactions, responsive layouts, and clear navigation between all features.
